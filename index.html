Play Musik

I made this so I could make music without learning how to compose.I don't play instruments. I don't know music theory. But I love creating.

The inspiration

Habbo Hotel — I grew up on it. What hooked me was the WIRED furniture. It's visual programming — connect blocks, things move. It taught me logic without me realizing it.

Incredibox — I loved it as a kid. Always wondered how it worked under the hood.

How it happened

I saw the vibecoding competition on Xiaohongshu. Rules: 100% offline, pure HTML/CSS/JS. I thought: "I have an idea. Let me actually build it."

So I built my own version of Incredibox. With synthetic sounds. Offline.

The problem

I tried recording my own sounds. Failed. Zero musical training.

So I used the Web Audio API to synthesize everything — kick drums, snares, melodies, all with code. No audio files. No samples. It worked.

7 kits. 79 sounds. All offline.

Click a character. They make a sound. The sound loops. You build a beat.

No account. No ads. No internet. Just pixels and music.

Why share it?

Xiaohongshu gave me a reason to finish. And creativity shouldn't require Wi-Fi.

— Built on nights and weekends. Just code.
